terms. Offering a commercial license requires the Maintainer to hold sufficient

commercial option unavailable for the whole project, because the Maintainer could

This agreement is the standard way to resolve that. It is modelled on the Apache
Software Foundation's Individual CLA, with the additional re-licensing grant that

## How to sign

Signing is automated. When you open your first pull request, a bot will comment
asking you to confirm. Reply on that pull request with **exactly**:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your signature is recorded in [`signatures/cla.json`](signatures/cla.json) in this
repository, along with your GitHub username, the pull request number, and the time.
You sign once; later pull requests are checked against that record automatically.

If you need to withdraw your agreement, email **vikoit07@gmail.com**. Withdrawal
applies to future Contributions only — the grants above are irrevocable for
Contributions already merged, because the project and its users have already
relied on them.
